A few things worth knowing

Before you make
something meaningful.

What exactly is a NIVA gift?

A personalised, interactive mini-website that someone can open as a gift — not just a greeting card or a message template.

How do I start creating a gift?

Choose a category, select a template, preview it, then choose Customise to open the gift editor.

Can I buy a gift right now?

Checkout is not active from the pricing buttons on this homepage. Do not send payment based on this prototype page.

Are uploaded photos and videos completely private?

Do not assume every production privacy feature is active just because the interface describes the intended experience. Review the Privacy & Safety page and avoid using sensitive personal media until production storage, deletion and deployment settings have been verified.

When will more templates be available?

The catalog is structured so more templates can be added to each category. The Love collection currently includes Eternal Love and Love Proposal. New templates can be added to the catalog data and registered with the editor and publishing backend.
