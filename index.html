What exactly is a NIVA gift?

A personalised, interactive mini-website that someone can open as a gift — not just a greeting card or a message template.

How do I start creating a gift?

Choose a template, preview it, then choose Customise to open the gift editor. After personalising, complete payment to get a unique shareable link.

Is payment required to create a gift?

Yes — you can customise and preview for free, but the unique gift link is generated only after successful payment. Each template has a fixed price.

Are my photos and memories private?

Yes. Your gift is encrypted with a password you choose. Only you and your recipient (who receives the password) can see the content.

Can I create multiple gifts?

Absolutely. One account allows you to create as many gifts as you like — each with its own unique link and password.

How big can my uploads be?

Photo galleries, videos and audio combined can be up to ~1 GB per gift. Drafts only store text; uploads are used when publishing.

What is the Special Letter for ₹39?

The Special Letter is our most affordable gift — one heartfelt letter with a beautiful opening animation. Nothing else, just pure feeling. Perfect for a small but meaningful gesture.
