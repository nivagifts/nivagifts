Privacy, rules
& safety.

NIVA is designed to help people create personal digital gifts. This page explains sensible privacy expectations, safe use and the rules we intend users to follow.

Important: This is a starter policy page for the current NIVA project, not legal advice or a claim that every operational policy is already implemented. Before public launch, update it with the real business contact, retention/deletion process, hosting details, payment provider and legally reviewed terms.

01 · Privacy

Privacy comes first

Personal gifts may contain names, messages, photos, videos and important dates. Only add information that you have a right to use and that the recipient would reasonably expect to receive.

  • Do not upload another person's private information without permission.
  • Do not include addresses, identity documents, passwords, financial details or other sensitive information in a gift.
  • Use a unique password for each private gift and do not share it publicly.
  • Anyone who receives both a gift link and its password may be able to open that gift. Share them carefully.
02 · Personal content

Your photos, words and memories

You remain responsible for the content you submit and must have permission to use it. Do not upload content that violates another person's privacy, copyright or other rights.

Before launch, NIVA must clearly publish what information is stored, where it is stored, how long it is retained, how a user can request deletion, and how backups are handled. Do not assume that closing a browser or deleting a local draft deletes a published gift from the server.

03 · Security

Security and private gift links

The current project includes browser-side encryption for the gift content and a password gate on the recipient page. Encryption does not make a link invulnerable: protect your password, use an up-to-date device and avoid opening private gifts on shared devices.

  • Never send your account password or gift password to someone claiming to be support.
  • Sign out after using a shared device.
  • Report a lost or exposed link/password through the official contact channel once NIVA publishes one.
  • Do not rely on a private link as a substitute for consent or permission to share the content.

Transparency: the final production service should document what metadata the server can access, how encryption keys are handled, and which service providers process data. Do not promise that nobody can ever access data until the deployed system and its operations have been independently checked.

04 · Rules

Rules for using NIVA

  • Use NIVA lawfully and respectfully.
  • Do not create gifts to threaten, harass, impersonate, blackmail or humiliate anyone.
  • Do not share intimate, private or embarrassing material without clear permission.
  • Do not upload malware, attempt to bypass passwords, probe other users' gifts or interfere with the service.
  • Do not use NIVA to infringe copyright, impersonate another person or distribute illegal content.
  • Do not attempt to access another user's account or gift without authorization.
05 · Safety

Make the surprise safe and welcome

A thoughtful gift should make someone feel respected. Avoid content that could put a person at risk, reveal their location, pressure them to respond, or expose a private relationship or personal circumstance.

  • Ask permission before using sensitive photos or sharing a gift with a group.
  • Do not use a gift link to track, pressure or repeatedly contact someone who does not want contact.
  • Keep minors' personal information and images especially protected.
  • If a gift contains an accidental disclosure, stop sharing the link and password and use the service's published support process when available.
06 · Your choices

Your choices and future updates

Users should be able to understand what they submit and how to request access, correction or deletion where applicable. The final version of this page should include a working support email, the legal business identity, an effective date, an update history, retention details, and any applicable consumer or privacy rights.

For now, this page is informational and does not itself create a support channel or automated deletion feature. Those must be connected and tested before public launch.

07 · Payments & refunds

Refund & Payment Protection Policy

Every Gift Matters. Every Payment Deserves Transparency.

NIVA aims to deliver the digital gift selected and purchased by each customer. If a verified technical issue prevents delivery or access, you can contact our support channel to request a review under this policy.

When you may request a refund

  • Payment successful, but no gift link: your payment is confirmed, but a gift link was not generated.
  • Gift link shows “Page Not Found”: the purchased gift cannot be opened because of a verified technical or delivery issue.
  • Verified delivery mismatch: the delivered website materially differs from the template preview you selected because of a technical or delivery error.
  • Another verified technical failure: a genuine NIVA-side problem prevents you from accessing the gift you purchased.

How to request a refund

Contact NIVA through our Instagram account: @niva.gifts. Explain the problem and provide genuine evidence so we can verify the claim.

  • Payment confirmation or transaction/order ID.
  • The gift link, if one was generated.
  • Screenshots or a screen recording showing the issue.
  • A short explanation of what happened.

We will check the payment record, selected template, delivered gift and evidence before deciding whether the request qualifies. Never send your account password, gift password, card PIN or OTP to support.

Refund processing time

Once a genuine claim is verified and approved, NIVA aims to initiate the eligible refund within 2 working days. This period allows time for payment and issue verification and for us to initiate the refund. The time for the money to appear in your bank account may depend on the payment provider and your bank, so the actual credit date may be later.

When a refund is not available

A refund is generally not available for a change of mind or personal design preference when the gift link has been generated successfully and the gift opens and works as described.

  • You dislike the selected theme, colours, fonts, animations or visual style.
  • You want a different template or design after purchase.
  • You change your mind after the gift has been delivered correctly.
  • The gift matches the selected preview and its advertised functionality, but you simply prefer a different appearance.

Please review the template preview and its details carefully before purchasing.

If the delivered gift differs from the preview

If the delivered website materially differs from the selected preview—for example, an advertised section or functionality is missing—send screenshots or a screen recording through Instagram. We will investigate whether this is a genuine technical or delivery error.

If the delivered gift matches the selected template and works correctly, a preference for different colours, fonts, themes or styling alone does not qualify for a refund.

Fair review and consumer rights

Refund requests are reviewed individually using the payment record and evidence provided. NIVA may investigate suspected fraudulent claims, but genuine claims will be assessed fairly. Nothing in this policy excludes any consumer rights that cannot legally be excluded.
